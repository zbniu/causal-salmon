# Tutoring Class Effect on Student Score Gains: Final Report

## Research Question
For the students who attended the tutoring class, did attending the tutoring class increase their score gain `z`? If so, by how much?

## Answer
**Yes, attending the tutoring class significantly increased score gains.**

## Key Finding
Students who attended the tutoring class gained approximately **1.11 points more** on the post-test than students who did not attend, holding their pre-test scores fixed by design through randomization.

## Statistical Evidence

### Treatment Effect Estimate
- **Point estimate:** 1.110 points increase in score gain
- **95% Confidence interval:** [0.685, 1.535] points
- **Interpretation:** We are 95% confident that the true treatment effect lies between 0.685 and 1.535 points

### Statistical Significance
- **t-statistic:** 5.259
- **p-value:** < 0.000001 (highly significant)
- **Conclusion:** The treatment effect is statistically significantly different from zero at the α = 0.05 level

### Effect Size
- **Cohen's d:** 0.257
- **Interpretation:** Small to moderate practical effect size

## Methodology
Analysis used an independent samples t-test with Welch's approximation, appropriate for this randomized controlled trial design with unequal group sizes. The analysis included all 2000 students with no missing data.

## Conclusion
The evidence strongly supports that attending the tutoring class causally increased student score gains. The estimated effect is approximately 1 point on the 0–100 scale, with a 95% confidence interval from 0.7 to 1.5 points.
