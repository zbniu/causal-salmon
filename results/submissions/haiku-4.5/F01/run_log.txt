SUMMARY OF KEY RESULTS
======================

Raw (Unadjusted) Treatment Effect: 5.4051 points
- Students attending tutoring gained 5.4051 more points on average
- p-value: < 0.000001 (highly significant)
- This estimate is confounded by selection bias

Adjusted Treatment Effect (controlling for start-of-term score): 3.7132 points
- After accounting for students' incoming ability level
- p-value: < 0.000001 (highly significant)
- Relationship with start-of-term score: 0.1918 points per point
- R-squared: 0.398 (model explains 39.8% of variance in score gain)

Selection Mechanism:
- Treated (attended): mean start-of-term score = 65.71
- Control (did not attend): mean start-of-term score = 56.89
- Difference: 8.82 points (students admitted to tutoring had much higher baseline scores)

Robustness:
- Stratified analysis by quartiles shows consistent treatment effect of ~3.7-3.9 points
- Mann-Whitney U test confirms significance of differences (p < 0.000001)
- All results consistent across multiple analytic approaches
